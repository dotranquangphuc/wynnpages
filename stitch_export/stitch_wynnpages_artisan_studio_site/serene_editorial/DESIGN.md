## Brand & Style

This design system embodies understated luxury, intellectual pause, and poetic warmth. Designed for an artisanal lifestyle publication, it prioritizes deliberate breathing room over density, tactile calm over technological frenzy, and editorial curation over utilitarian consumption.

The style harmonizes **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Translucency**. Layouts reflect high-end print publishing traditions—asymmetrical compositions, sweeping typographic scale, and deliberate margins—tempered by subtle frosted glass overlays that hint at physical parchment, vellum, and soft natural light. The aesthetic communicates mindfulness, quiet authority, and timeless curation for a discerning, culturally attuned readership.

## Colors

The palette draws deeply from raw ceramics, linen paper, dried flora, and crushed mineral pigments.

- **Primary (`#B85D43` Terracotta)**: A grounded, kiln-fired red-earth tone used selectively for focal accents, critical highlights, and intentional editorial emphasis. It should be applied with restraint to maintain high-luxury impact.
- **Secondary (`#8A9A86` Muted Sage)**: A dry, botanical gray-green that offers natural balance, soothing counterpoints, and secondary categorizations (such as culture, flora, or lifestyle badges).
- **Tertiary (`#D4B996` Warm Sand)**: A sun-warmed dune tone providing subtle warmth, grounding key border elements, dividers, and delicate active states.
- **Neutral (`#F9F6F0` Warm Cream)**: Replaces stark clinical white with the softness of unbleached heavy rag cotton paper, serving as the foundational atmosphere.

Text is rendered in `deep-charcoal` (`#1C1C1A`) and `charcoal-muted` (`#484742`) rather than pure black, ensuring high contrast without harsh digital sterility.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary drama of Playfair Display with the breezy, geometric clarity of Plus Jakarta Sans. 

- **Editorial Flourishes**: Headlines invite selective italicization—italicize contextual adjectives, literary emphasis, or French quotes to infuse warmth and bespoke print craft into digital surfaces.
- **Body & Legibility**: Body copy is set in Plus Jakarta Sans with generous line heights (`1.65` to `1.75`) to encourage sustained, unhurried longform reading. 
- **Micro-Copy & Metadata**: Category labels, dates, photo credits, and issue markers leverage tracked-out uppercase styling (`label-md`, `label-lg`) to balance the traditional serif headlines with clean Scandinavian modernity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors classical architectural proportion and high-fashion editorial grids.

- **Grid Structure**: A flexible 12-column system on desktop with ample `3.5rem` outer canvas borders and deep `2rem` gutters. Tablet adjusts to 8 columns with `2rem` outer margins; mobile resolves to a 4-column layout with `1.25rem` canvas protection.
- **Generous Voids**: White space is treated as an active narrative element, not vacant space. Spacing between feature sections expands to `space-xl` (and occasionally doubles to 8rem for cover transitions) to create cadence and contemplative pacing.
- **Asymmetric Offsets**: Featured essays and image galleries intentionally break symmetry—spanning 7 columns for imagery and 4 columns for accompanying body copy, tethered by an offset single-column white canal.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved organically through translucency and tactile layer stacking rather than synthetic box shadows.

- **Vellum Glassmorphism**: Navigation headers, persistent curation bars, and contextual overlays utilize semi-opaque surfaces (`rgba(249, 246, 240, 0.72)`) backed by a wide `16px` to `24px` backdrop blur and a whisper-light bottom border (`rgba(28, 28, 26, 0.05)`). This simulates heavy translucent drafting paper floating above typography and photography.
- **Ambient Ochre Shadows**: When physical elevation is necessary (e.g., floating cards, reading drawer sheets), avoid industrial grey shadows. Instead, cast soft, diffuse shadows tinted with warm pigment: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(60, 45, 30, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(60, 45, 30, 0.03)`.
- **Low-Contrast Organic Hairlines**: Structural boundaries and card frames rely on fine hairline strokes (`1px solid rgba(212, 185, 150, 0.4)`) that echo deckled book edges.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an architectonic, softly rounded silhouette (`roundedness: 1`). 

- Standard components (buttons, input fields, cards) feature subtle `0.25rem` (4px) curvature, preserving a grounded, printed-paper feel that avoids both cold, sharp brutalism and overly playful, bubbly pill-shapes.
- Media elements, art plates, and article card frames apply `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px) to soften photographic borders, evoking traditional beveled mat boards used in fine-art framing.
- Category tags, circular pull-buttons, and social curation badges may selectively use complete circular pill contours when standalone interaction signifiers are needed.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Terracotta background (`#B85D43`), crisp cream text (`#F9F6F0`), `0.25rem` border radius. Generous internal horizontal padding (`1.75rem`) with compact vertical padding (`0.75rem`). Hover transitions smoothly to Terracotta Soft (`#D97D64`) with a delicate lift.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent fill, framed by a `1px` subtle hairline in Sand (`#D4B996`). Text in Deep Charcoal (`#1C1C1A`). On hover, background washes with subtle Sand Surface (`#EFEAE1`).
- **Editorial Text Link**: Inline, self-styled text link accompanied by a micro-arrow or em-dash (`—`), underlined with a soft terracotta line positioned with `4px` offset.

### Cards & Editorial Modules
- **Story Card**: Flat or floating on `sand-surface` (`#EFEAE1`) with `rounded-lg` geometry. Images sit proud with a 4:5 or 16:10 aspect ratio. Metadata (`label-md`) sits above the Playfair Display story headline with `space-sm` separation.
- **Vellum Glass Card**: Elevated editorial quotes and audio player modules framed in translucent `vellum-glass` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and an organic border.

### Input Fields & Forms
- Minimalist open layout: Fields employ a recessed warm-cream fill or a single bottom hairline in Sand (`#D4B996`), shifting to Terracotta (`#B85D43`) upon active focus. Typography renders in `body-md`. Placeholder text appears in muted charcoal at 50% opacity.

### Chips & Badges
- Muted botanical or sand pills: Sage background (`#E4EBE1`) paired with Sage Dark text (`#435440`) or Sand background with Charcoal text. Set in `label-md` with `0.5rem` horizontal padding, signaling topics like *Architecture*, *Slow Living*, or *Essay*.

### Lists & Chapter Indexes
- Clean, generous horizontal rules (`rgba(212, 185, 150, 0.4)`) dividing entries. Each index item features an italic numeral (e.g., *01*, *02*), an expansive headline, and aligned right-hand reading time in `body-sm`.

### Pull Quotes & Footnotes
- Centered or asymmetric pull quotes featuring Playfair Display Italic (`quote-pull`) with a discreet vertical Terracotta hairline (`2px`) anchoring the quote on the left. Footnotes sit at the base of the page in `body-sm` muted charcoal with super-scripted serif numerals.
