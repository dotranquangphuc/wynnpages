Client Etiquette & FAQ

Everything you need to know.

Transparent guidance on appointment bookings, nail aftercare, and hygiene standards.

How long does a BIAB manicure last, and how often should I infill?

A structured BIAB (Builder in a Bottle) overlay typically maintains its strength and porcelain shine for 3 to 4 weeks. Because natural nails grow continuously, we recommend scheduling an infill appointment every 3 to 4 weeks to re-balance the apex and protect your stress zone from lifting or snapping.

Is BIAB or Gel-X damaging to my natural nail plate?

Never when applied and removed professionally. Damage in traditional salons occurs from over-filing the natural plate with aggressive drill bits or peeling off product. At our atelier, we work exclusively with high-grade, HEMA-free gels, zero abrasive thinning, and gentle soak-off protocols that preserve your natural keratin layers.

Do I need to book in advance or do you accept walk-ins?

To maintain our calm, whisper-quiet sanctuary atmosphere and dedicate unhurried one-on-one attention to each client, we operate primarily by appointment. We strongly recommend booking through our live reservation portal 1 to 2 weeks in advance, especially for evening and weekend appointments.

What is your sanitisation and hygiene standard?

Your health is our utmost priority. All surgical-grade stainless steel implements undergo multi-stage ultrasonic cleaning followed by hospital-grade autoclave sterilisation, sealed in pouches unsealed directly in front of you. Nail files, buffers, and pedicure sanding caps are strictly 100% single-use.

Can I purchase an Atelier Gift Certificate?

Yes. We offer beautifully presented digital gift vouchers valid for all bespoke services and retail botanical care products. Gift certificates can be arranged directly through our booking link or by contacting salon reception.
