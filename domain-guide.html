GoDaddy — Delegate Access

Open GoDaddy Delegate Page ↗
  1. 1

    Open GoDaddy's Delegate Access page

    Click account.godaddy.com/access ↗ and sign in to your GoDaddy account.

  2. 2

    Under "People who can access my account", click + Invite to Access

    Enter Name: WynnPages and Email: hello@wynnpages.com.au

  3. 3

    Select access level: "Domains" (or "Products & Domains") → Click Invite

    That's it! GoDaddy never shares your password or credit card with us — it only lets us point your domain to your new website.

How to Register Your Own Domain

  1. 1

    Buy the domain in your own name & card (~$15–$20/year)

    Delegate technical setup to hello@wynnpages.com.au

    Once registered, follow the GoDaddy or VentraIP tab above to invite hello@wynnpages.com.au (or email us your login) and our engineers will connect your website for free!
